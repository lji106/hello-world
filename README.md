## College application tracker

Open `college-tracker/index.html` in a browser (works on a phone too). It has three views:

- **My Colleges**: a Common App-style list with the application plan (EA/RD/Rolling) and decision date next to each school.
- **School Pages**: one page per school in its colors. Swipe left and right to move between schools. Each page has your major, undergrad size, admit rates (overall and out-of-state or in-state for you), deadlines, and notes.
- **Decisions**: a spreadsheet that files each school under Accepted, Deferred, Waitlisted, Rejected, Pending or Not submitted.

Edits save in your browser. Use **Backup** to copy your list to another device.
